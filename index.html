Our story

Who we are

Al Sultan Salon has been cutting hair in Al Nahyan for at least three years, and the reviews have never stopped coming. It is a calm, clean, black-and-gold room where regulars walk in as strangers and leave as friends.

We are a team that takes the craft seriously and the chat lightly. No rush, no shortcuts — just a barber who listens to what you want and a mirror that tells you he got it right.
